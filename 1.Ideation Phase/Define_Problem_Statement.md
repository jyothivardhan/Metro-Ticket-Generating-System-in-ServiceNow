# Phase 1: Ideation Phase - Problem Statement

## Project: Metro Ticket Generating System – ServiceNow

### 1. Context and Background
Metro transit networks in metropolitan areas carry hundreds of thousands of daily commuters. However, a significant bottleneck remains at station ticket counters and physical token vending machines:
- Passengers spend between 10 to 25 minutes during peak hours waiting in queues to purchase tokens or tickets.
- Physical paper tickets and plastic tokens cause material waste, recurring procurement costs, and high replacement overhead.
- Lost or damaged physical tokens cause commuter disputes and revenue leakages.
- Lack of self-service digital ticketing accessible on standard web/mobile browsers leaves commuters frustrated and leads to station congestion.

### 2. Problem Statement
**Primary Problem:** Commuters lack a fast, accessible, and self-service digital mechanism to book metro travel tickets dynamically, causing station congestion, long counter queues, manual fare calculation errors, and delays in boarding.

**Final Customer Problem Statement:**
> *"I am a daily metro commuter who needs to travel across transit stations quickly and conveniently. I am trying to purchase a travel ticket without standing in lengthy ticket counter queues, but there is no centralized, self-service digital ticketing portal available, which causes me to lose valuable commute time and makes me feel frustrated and delayed."*

### 3. Target Audience / Stakeholders
- **Daily Commuters & Passengers:** Need quick, queue-free ticket generation from any mobile/desktop browser.
- **Station Operations & Ticketing Staff:** Need streamlined passenger flow and real-time visibility into active tickets.
- **Transit Authority Administrators:** Require structured reporting, automated fare accounting, and ticket lifecycle tracking.

### 4. Proposed Objective
To architect and implement an automated **Metro Ticket Generating System** in **ServiceNow**, providing an intuitive Service Portal interface where commuters can select routes, calculate fares automatically, generate digital ticket passes with unique identification tokens (`MTKT0001001`), and manage ticket records in a custom application table.

# Phase 1: Ideation Phase - Empathy Map Canvas

## Project: Metro Ticket Generating System – ServiceNow

### Target Persona: The Daily Metro Commuter (Alex Johnson)
- **Role:** Working Professional / Student commuting via Metro Rail daily
- **Goal:** Reach workplace or college on time without waiting in ticketing queues

---

### 1. Think & Feel
- *"I am already late for my morning meeting; I hope the ticket counter queue isn't long."*
- *"Why can't I just buy a metro ticket from my mobile phone before arriving at the station?"*
- *"I need to know the exact fare and station route immediately."*
- *"I want confirmation that my ticket is valid and can be presented upon request."*

### 2. See
- Crowded station concourses and long lines at physical token vending machines.
- Digital signages displaying metro departure schedules.
- Service Portal homepage with "Book Metro Ticket" banner and commercial transit widgets.
- Clear dropdowns for origin station, destination station, and passenger count.

### 3. Hear
- Station announcements warning commuters about peak-hour crowds.
- Friends and colleagues discussing the convenience of mobile transit apps.
- Transit authority notices encouraging digital payments and paperless commuting.

### 4. Say & Do
- Accesses the ServiceNow Metro Service Portal on mobile or laptop.
- Chooses route, starting station, destination, and ticket type.
- Reviews auto-calculated fare.
- Submits booking request.
- Receives ticket number `MTKT0001001` and digital pass.

---

### Pains & Frustrations
- 15–20 minutes lost waiting at ticket counters.
- Lack of exact physical change or cash.
- Losing small plastic tokens or paper receipts.
- Confusing manual fare charts during peak hours.

### Gains & Desired Outcomes
- Instant ticket generation in under 30 seconds.
- Transparent, automated fare calculation.
- Eco-friendly paperless digital pass stored safely in ServiceNow.
- Guaranteed valid ticket before stepping onto the train platform.
